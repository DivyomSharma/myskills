# discovery-prep

One page, from `data/` rows and fresh public research:

1. **Company snapshot**: sourced facts only.
2. **Likely operational issues**: labelled hypotheses to test, not assertions.
3. **Discovery questions** by segment.
   - Facility/staffing: independent attendance verification per site; overtime and replacement reconciliation; payroll vs attendance vs client invoice; time spent on discrepancies; what breaks when the supervisor or ops manager is absent.
   - Distributors: stock discrepancies; order visibility across salespeople; credit and collections; purchasing; branch reporting.
4. **Stakeholder map**: owner, ops, finance; who sponsors, who blocks. Mark unknown.
5. **Relevant Verity capabilities**: from actual product state in `D:\Code\verity`; flag anything not built.
6. **Verify before quoting**: integrations, data volumes, user counts, compliance, existing tools (Tally etc.).
7. **Blueprint scope guard**: what the free Blueprint covers and does not.

Log `meeting` in `data/outcomes.csv`.
