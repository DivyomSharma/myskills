# Meta ads

Paid creatives work cold, in a feed, on a phone, in under 2 seconds. Copy and angle generation can use the
`ad-creative` and `ads` skills. This file governs how the creative looks.

## Organic vs paid

| | Organic | Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Reader | Follower, some context | Stranger, no context |
| First read | Can be a quiet statement | Must name the problem or outcome immediately |
| Product | Optional | Visible: UI, the industry prop or the outcome |
| CTA | Only on carousel end slides, as a micro link | Always: one accent pill or one underlined micro link |
| Copy on image | Up to the hero, support and micro | Hero plus at most 6 words of support; the rest goes in the primary text |

## Specs

- Format: 4:5, 1080 x 1350 (feed and Instagram). Make a 9:16 variant for Stories and Reels placements, keeping type out
  of the top 250px and the bottom 340px.
- Primary text: hook in the first 125 characters. Headline (below the image): up to 40 characters. Description: up to 30.
- Hero at least 88px. Nothing under 24px. Test at 360px wide.

## Creative angles (one idea, several angles)

| Angle | Hero pattern | Archetype | Example in Verity's voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain-led | The reader's current situation as a question or a fact | A or B | "Still running the business on WhatsApp?" |
| Outcome-led | The state after Verity | B or D | "Know what needs attention before it costs you." |
| Product-led | What the UI shows, in one line | B or C | The panel's real attention rows |
| Industry-led | The industry's own sentence from `content/` | G | "The shelf says one thing. The sheet says another." |
| Comparison | Many tools to one record | F | "Five places to look. One record." |

Make each idea in at least 3 angles. The visual system stays identical; the angle changes the hero and the object.

## CTA treatment

- Paid default: one pill, 64px tall, accent `#0a84ff` fill, white text at 28px/500, radius 32. It sits on the bottom
  margin, left-aligned with the hero, or right-aligned to the margin when the left is taken by the mark. Label: a verb
  plus an object ("See how it works", "Book a walkthrough"), at most 4 words.
- Editorial alternative: an uppercase underlined micro link under the hero, as Intercom does. Use one or the other,
  never both.
- The CTA must be visible but not the loudest thing: hero first, object second, CTA third.
