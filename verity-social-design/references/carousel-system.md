# Carousel system

A carousel is one designed artefact cut into 4:5 slides, not N separate posts.

## Narrative grammar

HOOK → PROBLEM → INSIGHT → CONSEQUENCE → SOLUTION → PRODUCT → PROOF / EXAMPLE → CTA

- Use the fewest slides that land the idea. Typical: 5-7. Merge beats when one slide can do both jobs (insight and
  consequence often merge).
- Each slide has exactly one job and one statement.
- The cover (HOOK) must feel incomplete without a swipe: a statement that sets up a turn, or a crop that obviously
  continues to the right.
- PROOF needs real evidence. Without it, use EXAMPLE: one real UI row or workflow step at macro scale.
- The CTA slide is the only slide with a CTA.

## Continuity devices (use at least two per carousel)

1. **Fixed type anchor:** the hero cap line sits on the same y (200) on every slide. Copy never jumps around.
2. **The record line:** a 2px hairline at a fixed y runs across the whole carousel, crossing slide seams. Facts are
   nodes on it. Before Verity the nodes are muted and the line is broken (dashed); from the SOLUTION slide the line is
   solid and the nodes connect. The narrative is drawn in one object.
3. **Carried object:** a UI panel or prop straddles a seam, half on one slide and half on the next, so the swipe reveals
   it.
4. **Scale progression:** the same panel shown at context scale, then cropped to one row at macro scale.
5. **Fixed furniture:** the lockup top-left on the cover only. The slide count "n / N" sits at the top-right margin on the
   lockup's line on every slide, so objects bleeding off the bottom never cover it. The lockup returns bottom-left on the
   CTA slide.

## Production

- Design on one wide canvas (N x 1080 by 1350), then export each 1080-wide slice. Anything crossing a seam is then exact.
- Grayscale composition pass first: boxes for type, line and object on the wide canvas.
- Seams: never put a word across a seam. Objects may cross seams; type never does.
- On a wide canvas, "bleeding off the right edge" means spilling into the next slide. Make a carried object end exactly
  on the next seam, unless the next slide is designed around its remainder.
- Every slide still has to pass the single-creative quality gate on its own.
